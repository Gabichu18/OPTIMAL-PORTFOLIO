Distribution of annualized returns over the horizon

Re-runs on every weight change. Bootstrap replays real months from the estimation window in random 6-month blocks, keeping crashes, fat tails and co-movement; each asset's average is shifted to its forward expected return. Before an asset listed, its months are back-filled from its beta to the S&P 500. Log-normal uses only expected return and volatility. Teal figures are the max-Sharpe portfolio. In today's euros, every future value is divided by (1 + inflation)years, so the chart shows purchasing power; contributions stay fixed in nominal euros, so their real value shrinks over time. Returns are modelled in USD, and the euros label assumes EUR/USD stays flat.

Grey dots are 4,000 random portfolios. The curve is the efficient frontier under your weight cap; the dashed CML runs from the T-bill rate through the max-Sharpe portfolio. Hover or tap points for detail.

Downside deviation counts only months below your minimum acceptable return (MAR), currently a year. The curve is the mean–downside frontier; the max-Sortino and min-downside portfolios are optimized on the portfolio's own semicovariance. VaR and CVaR are historical, monthly, 95%. Omega = gains above MAR ÷ shortfalls below it.

Risk contribution = weight × the asset's covariance with the whole portfolio ÷ portfolio variance; contributions add up to 100%. An asset whose amber bar is much longer than its grey bar adds more risk than its capital share suggests. Risk parity sets every contribution equal, which is how many institutional multi-asset funds are built.

Alpha generated

Realized alpha is the intercept of a regression of each portfolio's monthly return above the T-bill on the S&P 500's return above the T-bill (Jensen, 1968), using today's weights over the estimation window. It is the return the market exposure does not explain. The t-statistic says whether it is distinguishable from luck: above 2 is significant at 95%. The chart compounds that unexplained return month by month: a rising line is alpha being earned, a falling one is alpha being lost. Same caveats as the backtests: the weights and the 32 assets were chosen with hindsight.
